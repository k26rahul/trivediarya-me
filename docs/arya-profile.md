### English Classes (Current Offering)
She teaches a structured Spoken English course designed to help students think clearly, speak confidently, and communicate professionally.

#### Class Structure (40-minute sessions, 6 days a week)
| Segment | Duration | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Our Reading Ritual** | 5-7 min | Reading a well-written newspaper article - practices phonics, punctuation, and essential pauses |
| **The Spotlight** | 25-30 min | Student pitches ideas, presentations, or anything they would like - builds confidence and professional English |
| **Net Practice** | Remaining | Classroom grammar lessons and practice |

- **The Screen Test:** 15-minute recorded presentation on an assigned topic; evaluated on fluency, word choice, grammar, and presentation. Recordings are shared back with students to track growth.
- **Written Tests:** Grammar and vocabulary tests
- **Monthly Tests:** Covering classroom lessons
